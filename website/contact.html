Common questions

Is Remedy a medical device?

No. Remedy helps patients read their own papers and keep to the plan their doctors wrote. It doesn't replace their doctor and hasn't been clinically validated.

Where does patient data live?

On the patient's iPhone. The plan isn't synced to any server. Backups are one password-locked file the patient keeps.

Who reads the papers?

The patient picks: the on-device model, Apple's Private Cloud Compute, their own Mac, or a cloud AI with their own key. Before a page goes to a cloud AI, names and record numbers are blacked out and the patient sees the page.

What does a pilot need from us?

The surgery type, sample papers your patients go home with (patient details removed), and a contact on the care team. We test Remedy on those sample papers with you first.
