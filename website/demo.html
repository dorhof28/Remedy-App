Demo library

Screens, sample papers and demo patients for showing Remedy. Every patient here is made up.

    Ruth Callahan's four papers, marked SAMPLE. Add them to Remedy through Add papers to show a live read.

      Each one opens from Settings, Demo mode in the app, or with the launch argument shown.

        In the app

        1. Open Settings, Demo mode and turn it on.
        2. Under Start here, tap HSS knee replacement. Ruth's four papers load and the clock moves to day 4 at home.
        3. Use Pretend today is to move the day. Go back before surgery to see the red Eliquis card on Today.
        4. For a live read, add D1 to D4 from Files through Add papers. Set Read papers with to Expected answers if there's no AI set up.
        5. Turn demo mode off. The real plan comes back and nothing from the demo stays.

        On day 4 at 2 PM: acetaminophen due now, a missed 9 AM apixaban, oxycodone "You can take 1 now", home PT at 11 AM, and red, blue and green cards on Papers.

        In the Simulator

        Debug builds only. This opens the Simple look on day 4 at 2 PM.

        xcrun simctl launch booted com.dorronhoffman.Remedy \
  -demoScenario hssKnee -demoTime 14:00 \
  -appLookMode simple -appLook simple \
  -skipPermissionPrompts YES

        Pick the day: −5 is five days before surgery, 0 is surgery day, 4 is the default, 14 is two weeks out.

        xcrun simctl spawn booted defaults write \
  com.dorronhoffman.Remedy demoPlanDay -int -5
