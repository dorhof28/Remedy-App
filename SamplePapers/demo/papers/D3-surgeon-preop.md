### ^__**Preparing for Your Surgery**__

Your surgery is scheduled for **Thursday, November 12, 2026**. Please arrive at **6:00 AM** at the HSS Lakeside Main OR, 400 Harbor St, 4th Floor. Check in at Surgical Admitting.

The hospital will call you the business day before your surgery if your arrival time changes. If you have not heard from us by 3:00 PM the day before, please call 555-0142.

**Eating and drinking**
- Do not eat anything after midnight the night before your surgery. This includes gum, mints and hard candy.
- You may have clear liquids (water, apple juice, Gatorade, black coffee or tea without milk) up until 2 hours before your arrival time.

**Skin preparation**
- Shower with Hibiclens (chlorhexidine 4%) soap the night before surgery and the morning of surgery. Do not use it on your face or private areas.
- Do not shave the surgical area for 3 days before surgery.
- Do not use lotion, powder or deodorant after your shower on the morning of surgery.
- Remove all jewelry, piercings and nail polish.

**Medications**
- Follow the preoperative medication instructions from your medical doctor.
- Stop Eliquis (apixaban) 3 days prior to surgery.
- Stop all NSAIDs (Advil, Motrin, Aleve, Mobic, Celebrex) 4 days prior to surgery.

**What to bring**
- Photo ID and insurance card
- A list of your medications
- Your rolling walker
- Inhalers, eye drops and CPAP machine if you use them
- Loose comfortable clothing and flat shoes with a closed back

**Going home**
- Most patients go home 1 day after surgery.
- You must have a responsible adult take you home. You may not leave by taxi, car service or rideshare alone.
- Please pick up your rolling walker before your surgery date.
