# Before Your Surgery - Standard Instructions
Northfield General Hospital (SAMPLE)
Phone: 555-0188

Printed: January 4, 2027

This handout is for all surgery patients.

## Blood thinners
Stop all blood thinners 7 days before surgery, including aspirin, clopidogrel (Plavix), warfarin (Coumadin), apixaban (Eliquis).

## Vitamins and supplements
Stop vitamins and supplements 7 days before surgery.

## Pain medicine
You may take ibuprofen until 3 days before surgery.
